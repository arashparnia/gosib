# Code of conduct

PechPech welcomes participation from people of every background and experience level. These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces, including interactions where someone represents the project.

## Participate respectfully

- Explain technical disagreements with evidence and consider the other person's perspective.
- Help newcomers, give constructive feedback and respect people's names, identities and boundaries.
- Keep criticism about the work. Do not insult, intimidate, discriminate against or harass people.
- Do not publish someone's personal information, private correspondence or credentials without permission.
- Do not use sexualized language or imagery, unwanted sexual attention, threats or repeated unwanted contact.

## Report a concern

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer, Arash Parnia, at **arashparnia@gmail.com**. Include links to the relevant project content and a concise description. Share only the personal information needed to understand the report. If the report concerns the maintainer or abusive GitHub content, use [GitHub's abuse-reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

Security vulnerabilities use the separate [security reporting procedure](SECURITY.md). Do not publish exploit details in a conduct report or public issue.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews reports and may request clarification, remove inappropriate content, issue a warning, limit participation or ban a participant. The response should reflect the seriousness, impact and pattern of the conduct. Reports are handled as privately as practical; the project cannot promise absolute confidentiality or a fixed response time.

Participants may ask for clarification or reconsideration through the same private contact. The maintainer also follows these expectations.
